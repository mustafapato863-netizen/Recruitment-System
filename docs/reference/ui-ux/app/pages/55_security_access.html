Every screen starts with a boundary.

RecruitFlow resolves the organization, role, record relationship, field sensitivity, and action permission before it renders data or accepts a mutation.

Three journeys through the same systemRole guidance is easier to remember when the handoff is told as a story.
Role playbook
R
Sarah, the RecruiterMoves people forward without losing evidence.

Sarah starts with her assigned queue. She does not search the whole company by default; she works the openings and applications she owns.

  1. 01
    Find the next actionMy Tasks shows overdue screening, interviews, and offer work assigned to Sarah.
  2. 02
    Prepare the candidateShe can create or edit candidate data, review consent, and connect the person to an opening.
  3. 03
    Move with a reasonStage changes require the allowed transition and keep an audit trail.
  4. 04
    Hand off evidenceInterviewers receive their scorecards; the hiring manager receives a decision-ready application.
Sarah hands off toInterviewer for evidence · Hiring Manager for evaluation · Offer Approver for compensation.
HM
Ahmed, the HR ManagerProtects the process while making the business decision.

Ahmed opens the Command Center and sees organization-level risk: pending vacancy approvals, salary-band exceptions, overdue feedback, and joining readiness.

  1. 01
    Validate the needHe reviews headcount, department, branch, budget, and approval evidence before approving a vacancy.
  2. 02
    Guide the teamHe sees the recruiting funnel and can unblock ownership, workflow, or missing evidence.
  3. 03
    Protect sensitive fieldsHR salary and PII visibility is available only inside the organization policy and audit boundary.
  4. 04
    Approve or request changesEvery decision names the reason and sends a clear next task to the owner.
Ahmed hands off toRecruiter for execution · Offer Approver for package review · Final Approver for readiness.
A
The approval cycleOne decision, several accountable owners.

The cycle never jumps from draft to final approval. Each gate receives its evidence, records its decision, and creates the next owner.

  1. 01
    RequestHiring Manager submits headcount need.
  2. 02
    HR reviewHR Manager approves or requests changes.
  3. 03
    Offer reviewOffer Approver checks compensation.
  4. 04
    Final gateFinal Approver confirms readiness.
Cycle ruleBlocked evidence stops the next transition; audit history remains visible to authorized roles.
Five authorization layersA request is allowed only when all relevant layers pass.
  1. 1
    Tenant boundaryorganizationId comes from the authenticated session, never from a trusted browser field.
  2. 2
    Role permissionRequire a named permission such as APPLICATION_VIEW or APPROVE_OFFERS.
  3. 3
    Relationship scopeCheck assigned recruiter, hiring team, interviewer assignment, or approver ownership.
  4. 4
    Field sensitivityMask salary, PII, and documents independently from the parent record.
  5. 5
    Audit and reasonRecord sensitive reads and irreversible decisions with actor, object, reason, and time.
